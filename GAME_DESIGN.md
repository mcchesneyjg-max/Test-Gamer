# City Siege — Game Design

**Pitch:** Build a pixel city and its supply chains, then survive massive siege waves when the enemy arrives.

## Two-phase loop

1. **Build phase** — Expand your settlement, connect producers to consumers, stockpile food and materials, and prepare defenses while time still allows.
2. **Siege phase** — Enemy waves attack from the map edge; your economy must keep feeding the fight while walls, towers, and troops hold the line.

Win by surviving the siege with your city intact. Lose if critical buildings fall or your population is wiped out.
